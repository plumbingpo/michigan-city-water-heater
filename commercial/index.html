Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards from bad venting, scalding at the tap, bacterial growth in tanks kept too cool, and costly water damage from a failed tank. Michigan City Water Heater provides commercial installations and inspections that cover venting and combustion air, gas piping sizing, relief valve discharge piping, expansion tanks, thermostatic mixing valves, drain pans, seismic strapping where local code calls for it, and the permit and inspection process. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

Good records matter almost as much as good repairs. Every account gets a service binder with detailed service logs, temperature readings, and relief valve test results, giving you proof of due diligence for landlords, insurers, and inspectors. Equipment that works hardest, in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms, gets a more frequent visit schedule.

Emergency Service

Fast Commercial Water Heater Service

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Michigan City Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Michigan City and surrounding areas, and we schedule planned work after hours or before opening so your doors stay open.

Failed gas valves, burned-out elements, ignition faults, tripped high-limit switches, leaking tanks, stalled recirculation pumps, tankless lockouts: we see all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and restoring hot water fast, and in a multi-heater system we isolate the failed unit so the others carry the load. Once it's running, we put maintenance in place to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your facility files.

Ongoing Protection

Proactive Water Heater Care for Businesses

A maintenance program from Michigan City Water Heater is built around your equipment, how hard the system works, and your budget. Visits include anode checks and tank flushing, descaling of tankless heat exchangers, relief valve testing, burner, flue, and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at key fixtures. Next to an emergency water heater replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints.
